It's Too Expensive

Isolate the True Objection

What to Say

When you say it's expensive, is it that you don't see the full value yet, or is it strictly a cash flow timing issue for this quarter? I ask because I have different solutions for each.

Why It Works

Separates value perception from budget timing. Without this diagnostic question, you risk discounting prematurely when price wasn't the actual blocker.

It's Too Expensive

Best Option Anchoring

What to Say

"You're right that this isn't the cheapest option on the market; it's the one you'll never have to replace. Cheap solutions get expensive fast. Would you rather invest properly once or pay repeatedly for fixes?"

Why It Works

Anchoring contrasts the recurring pain of failed purchases against upfront cost. Works effectively with buyers seeking long-term reliability.

"I need to think about it"

Uncovering Hidden Doubts

What to Say

"Of course, taking time to think is a smart move. Just so I can help: what specific factor is still weighing on your mind? That way I can provide that exact detail right now so you don't waste time researching."

Why It Works

""I need to think about it"" suele ocultar una duda concreta. Al pedir el criterio de decisión, transforma una evasiva vaga en un punto accionable.

Pros & Cons Reality Check

What to Say

Engages the analytical buyer in their own logical framework and surfaces hidden objections to resolve immediately.

Impulsive Buyer
No tengo tiempo

Respetar y microcomprometer

What to Say

Justo por eso te llamo: porque sé que tu tiempo es lo más valioso. No te pido 30 minutos, te pido 4. Si en esos 4 minutos no ves algo que te ahorre tiempo, cuelgo y no vuelvo a insistir. ¿Trato hecho?

Why It Works

Lowers commitment threshold to an easy yes and leverages their limited time as a compelling reason to evaluate efficiency.

Ya trabajo con otro

El segundo par de ojos

What to Say

"I appreciate that you already have a trusted vendor; that shows you value this service. I'm not asking you to switch. I propose being your backup option: if your vendor ever bottlenecks or raises prices, you'll already know us. What do you have to lose by keeping us on your radar?"

Why It Works

Elimina la amenaza inmediata de cambio y se posiciona como plan B de bajo riesgo, ideal para clientes que temen equivocarse al cambiar.

Analytical Buyer
No me interesa

Validar y girar

What to Say

Lo entiendo, y tiene todo el sentido no interesarte por algo que aún no sabes cómo te ayuda. La mayoría de mis mejores clientes me dijeron exactamente eso al principio. ¿Puedo hacerte una sola pregunta antes de que decidas?

Why It Works

Validates the objection rather than confronting it. Social proof showing "our best clients felt the same way" reopens conversation naturally.

Planta una semilla de duda cuantificada y le da al analítico una salida digna, lo que paradójicamente aumenta su interés.

Preparar al mensajero

What to Say

Muy bien. Para que la conversación con esa persona sea fácil, diez centavos: ¿qué es lo que más le importaría a ella? Así te preparas justo ese argumento y llegas con la lista de respuestas antes de que te la pregunte.
