This page explains what cookies are, which cookies NubeParaPymes.online uses, and how you can manage them. It accompanies our Privacy Policy, which covers the general processing of your data.

1. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores in your browser when you visit. Cookies allow a website to remember your actions and preferences for a period of time, so you do not have to configure them again on each visit or when navigating between pages.

2. Types of cookies we use

Necessary cookies

These are essential for the website to work correctly, for example to remember your session between pages. They do not require your consent because they are indispensable for basic website operation.

Analytics cookies (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages visitors use, how long they stay, and where they come from. This information is collected in aggregated, anonymous form.

Advertising cookies (Google AdSense)

We display ads through Google AdSense, which uses cookies for personalized advertising based on browsing history. Google and its advertising partners may use these cookies on this and other websites.

3. Third-party cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party services appearing on our pages, mainly Google AdSense for personalized advertising and Google Analytics for browsing statistics. We do not directly control these cookies. Please see Google's cookie policy for more information.

4. Managing or disabling cookies

You can manage, block, or delete cookies in your browser settings. The process depends on your browser:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data.
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy and security → Cookies and site data.
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy.
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions.

You can also opt out of Google's personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. Blocking all cookies may affect some website features.
