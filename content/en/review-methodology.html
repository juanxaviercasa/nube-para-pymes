At NubeParaPymes.online, every software recommendation follows a clear, consistent review methodology. This page explains how we evaluate tools, which criteria we use, and how we verify prices, limits, and features, so you know what supports each guide, comparison, and recommendation.

How we test each tool

Before recommending software, we test it firsthand. Our methodology aims to reflect real small-business use, beyond the promises on a vendor's sales page.

  • We create a real account using a free plan or trial, depending on the provider.
  • We use the main everyday functions: creating contacts, organizing tasks, generating reports, and checking actual limits.
  • We take real screenshots of the platform in use. We do not use generic illustrations or artificial images of interfaces we have not tested.

Evaluation criteria

We compare tools using consistent criteria rather than subjective impressions:

  • Ease of use: how quickly someone without technical knowledge can get started.
  • Value for money: what the free plan actually includes and which features require payment.
  • Actual limits: permitted users, contacts, and projects, verified on the provider's official website.
  • Spanish-language support: whether the interface and customer service are available in Spanish.
  • Learning curve: how long it takes to master basic functions.
  • Integrations: how well a tool connects with software a small business may already use.

Keeping reviews current

Software prices and plan limits change frequently. We verify figures directly on each provider's official website before publishing and periodically review articles to correct information that has changed. This matters because free plans, user limits, and included features often change.

If you find an outdated price or limit, please tell us through our contact page. We correct it once confirmed.

Transparency and monetization

To keep this platform free and operating, we use affiliate links. If you implement a system through us, we earn a commission. Our comparisons, however, rely on hard data, actual prices, and objective stress tests.

Our methodology strictly separates technical assessment from commercial relationships. We first conduct stress tests and architecture analysis; only after issuing a data-based verdict do we add affiliate links, if available.

What we do not do

Trust is central to our methodology:

  • We do not accept payment for positive reviews.
  • There are no paid rankings. Recommendation order follows these criteria, not the size of a commission.
  • We do not recommend software we have not tested ourselves.

Have a suggestion?

If you would like us to review a specific tool or find outdated information, use our contact page. We read every message and use suggestions to improve our methodology and future comparisons.

Authorship and editorial review

Authorship: the Nube para Pymes editorial team. We write and update guides based on usefulness to small businesses.

Technical review: Juan Cabello Salirrosas, the website's editorial lead. He reviews the clarity of criteria, consistency of comparisons, and whether cited data comes from official sources or providers' pages.

Updates: we review prices, limits, features, and links when we identify relevant changes. If a tool changes its terms, we state the verification date or recommend checking directly with the provider.
