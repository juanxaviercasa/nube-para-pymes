At NubeParaPymes.online, we respect your privacy and want you to understand what information we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have.

1. Website operator

NubeParaPymes.online is an independent blog offering software guides and comparisons for small businesses. For questions about this policy, please use our contact page.

2. Information we collect

We collect limited information, mainly through:

  • Contact forms: your name and email address, only when you voluntarily write to us.
  • Newsletter subscriptions (if you subscribe): your email address.
  • Article comments (if enabled): your name, email address, and comment.
  • Browsing data: automatically through cookies and analytics tools (see section 4).

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for commercial purposes unrelated to this website.

3. Cookies

This website uses first-party and third-party cookies to improve your browsing experience, analyze traffic, and display relevant advertising. A cookie is a small file stored in your browser. You can disable or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Some website features may not work correctly if you disable all cookies.

4. Google AdSense advertising

This website displays advertisements through Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party provider, uses cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's advertising cookie enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this website and/or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings.

5. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with the website, including popular pages, time spent, and traffic sources. This information is aggregated and anonymous; it does not identify individual users.

6. Affiliate links

Some links in our articles are affiliate links. If you click one and subsequently purchase the recommended software, we may receive a commission at no additional cost to you. This does not influence our recommendations: we only suggest tools we consider useful for small businesses, based on actual testing as explained in our review methodology.

7. Third-party websites

Our articles may link to external websites, such as official websites of software we review. We are not responsible for their privacy practices or content. We recommend reading their own privacy policies.

8. Your rights

Under Peru's Personal Data Protection Law No. 29733, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request cancellation or deletion of your data.
  • Object to processing of your data for specific purposes.

To exercise these rights, write to us through our contact page.

9. Information security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect, but no website can guarantee absolute security. You use this website at your own risk.

10. Changes to this policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The last-updated date at the top of this page identifies the latest version.
